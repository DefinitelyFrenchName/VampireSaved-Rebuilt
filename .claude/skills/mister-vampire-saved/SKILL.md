## 2.1 What the roster demands (`mister_fit.md`)

- [MSV-1] **The demand figures are merged-m6's — quote them WITH their freeze.** Measured on the original's build at merged-m6: PRG live to `PRG:0x4D10F3` plus the 30-byte facing-alias pin at `PRG:0x5FFF00` (deficit `0xD10F4` = 836 KB against a flat 4 MB); QSound extension 918 KB in DSP banks `0x80-0x8E`; group C = 16 MB of members. The four CEILINGS (`0xEE73`, `0xFFDB`, `0x8E57F0`, `0x5FFF1E`) were RE-DERIVED from the current freeze by a gate on every run; the wide_ext HIGH-WATER MARK `0x4D10F3` was NOT gate-frozen and merged-m9 shifted the extension. This repository's figures are its own build's: re-derived by a gate, quoted with the build they were measured on. *(adapted: the original named its fit gate, `tests/audit_mister_map_fit.sh`.)*
- [MSV-2] **The roster's art is 13x every blank tile in vanilla's 32 MB put together** (6.39 MB of live group-C tiles vs 0.49 MB blank, and blank is only an upper bound). There is no fit-inside-stock variant and no tenant-dropping variant (Pyron's band alone is 3.5x the blank total): a MiSTer build of this roster REQUIRES a GFX map wider than 32 MB.
- [MSV-3] **Two counts of "group-C live codes" circulate and are different quantities** (measured on the original's build): 52,347 codes / 6.39 MB from the as-built WRITE SET (`mister_fit.md` §3) vs 45,736 + 6,245 from the NON-BLANK census of the shipped members (`mister_map.md` §1, through 14z-113) — 366 tiles the build writes as all-`00`/`FF`. The census moved with the build afterwards (the original's `mister_core.md` §2 records 45,736 + 6,272 at 14z-117). Neither governs placement ([MSC-22]).
- [MSV-4] **The frozen extents are what a moved extent has to be re-frozen AGAINST, deliberately.** Tenant art may grow freely inside the existing 16 MB (on the original's build a code above `0xEE73` or `0xFFDB` overflows nothing), and the fit gate still goes RED on purpose — re-derive, re-freeze, never absorb. *(adapted: the original named its gate, `audit_mister_map_fit`.)*

## 2.2 The placement map (`mister_map.md` §5)

- [MSV-5] **The map, in bytes**, measured on the original's build at its freeze: bank 0 — PRG 6 MB at 0, VRAM `0x600000`, ORAM `0x640000`, work RAM `0x648000`, Z80 `0x658000`, QSound PCM-high `0x6E0000` (1 MB window), group-C obj bank 5 `0x7E0000` (8 MB region), **131,072 B free**; bank 1 — PCM-low 8 MB at 0, group-C obj bank 4 `0x800000`, **EXACTLY FULL**; banks 2 and 3 untouched to the byte. Total reserved 63.875 MB of 64: **slack 0.125 MB, all of it in bank 0** (the 0.708 MB once claimed sized the group-C banks by footprint and is RETRACTED). A rebuilt map is censused afresh ([MJC-24], [MJC-50]).
- [MSV-6] **QSound is SPLIT across two banks on `pcm_addr[23]`** (the original's placement) — stock DSP banks `0x00-0x7F` stay at bank 1 offset 0 byte-identical to stock, banks `0x80+` (exactly what the profile added) go to bank 0. Forced twice over: QSound whole in bank 1 overflows by 0.9375 MB best case, and a slot cannot exceed 8 MB anyway ([MSC-19]). The high window is masked to its 1 MB on BOTH sides; `vsw.22m` is simply not mapped.
- [MSV-7] **Group C is one obj bank per SDRAM bank, and the assignment is deliberate** (the original's placement): obj bank **4** (the three fighter bands — in-match traffic) → bank 1, whose headroom was measured; obj bank **5** (select/wheel art, cold in a match) → bank 0, so its load lands on the select screen.
- [MSV-8] **The QSound trim is MANDATORY, not an optimisation**: mapped verbatim (WIDE v1 declared sizes) the WIDE `.rom` is 73,670,720 B, past the 26-bit `ioctl_addr`, with a firmware start word silently WRAPPED to 6400. Measured on the original's build: placed at 8.9375 MB (`0x8F0000`, banks `0x00-0x8E`) it is **66,265,152 B** with header words **6144 / 6400 / 15552 / 64704**, every region 1 KiB-aligned, held byte for byte by a gate. *(adapted: the original named its gate, `test_mister_mra_map`.)*
- [MSV-9] **Bank 0 needs SEVEN streams** (on the original's placement), **and upstream's `ram1_Nslots` family stops at five**: `jtframe_ram1_7slots.v` (ruled option A, 2026-08-23) is a mechanical sibling of `ram1_5slots.v`, placed with its family and pulled by `cores/cps2w/cfg/game.yaml` alone ([MSC-3]).
- [MSV-10] **The load is MEASURED on the WIDE image and it is GO — with the caveat stated** (measured on the original's WIDE image): bank 0 runs 40,717 accesses/frame through the select screen (32.9% of ceiling; the redirect costs ~1,000/frame, ~2.5%), whole-run peak 54,363 (43.9%); bank 1 peaks at 15,496 (12.5%) with the fighter art sharing it with PCM; zero `SDRAM reads clashed`. Bank 1's figure rests on ONE replay, ONE tenant, one opponent.
- [MSV-11] **What cannot grow: the group-C ROMSET REGION** (on the original's placement). A fifth group-C member or anything past 16 MB overflows immediately — bank 1 has zero free and bank 0 has 131,072 B — and there is nowhere for the excess to go.
- [MSV-12] **On the original's `cps2w` work RAM is SDRAM bank 0 byte `0x648000`; `0x600000` — the reference core's work RAM — is VRAM there.** The simulation driver selects the constant per core and PRINTS it; the stale one turned the core-vs-core inertness gate red 101 frames of 101 with the RTL innocent ([MSC-41]). A core that re-packs bank 0 differently derives its own constant from its RTL. *(adapted: the original named its driver, `tools/run_sim_jtcps2.sh` `--core`, and its gate, `test_mister_wide_inert`.)*
- [MSV-13] **The `$400000-$40000F` reservation is load-bearing THREE ways**: ROM on `jtcps2w`, ROM-shadowed on FBNeo, readable registers on MAME — a divergence unobservable only because nothing may live there. The romset's allocator must honour it (the original's `wide_ext` starts at `0x400010`).
- [MSV-14] **The synthesis cannot go stale silently**: a check re-derives every figure the MiSTer synthesis (`mister_core.md`) states from the constants the gates freeze, on every static tier; the rendered page is never committed. *(adapted: the original's check was `tools/mk_mister_page.py --check`, 17 checks, run by `run_all_static`.)*

## 2.5 Field test and triage (`mister_field.md`)

- [MSV-31] **Hardware answers what simulation cannot — pixels seen, voices heard, real SDRAM/timing/analog — and every hardware verdict so far is a person at a CRT.** No frame and no audio has been captured off the board; the field test IS this project's cheapest pixel oracle, and a skill or report that says "verified on hardware" means "judged by eye".
- [MSV-32] **Never field-test without the control**: `[STOCK CONTROL]` on the same `.rbf`. STOCK boots + WIDE fails = OURS (highest-value report); STOCK fails too = NOT ours (bitstream, SDRAM module, card, video chain). Verify the `.rbf` hash and every MRA part BEFORE the card leaves the desk.
- [MSV-33] **Report the failure MODE, and time it**: no sync vs black vs RAM-test loop — a ~26.5 s loop (~1,580 frames at 59.6374 Hz; a phone stopwatch is a valid instrument) is the pre-D5 decryption signature, any other period is a different fault and the number IS the diagnostic; a name-screen reboot after a brief check list = a CPU EXCEPTION (code at `RAM:$FF0000`), the gold full self-test = cold start/watchdog. The download is not comparable (HPS speed).
- [MSV-34] **FIELD REPORTS ARE RECORDINGS** ([VSR-19]; the original's CLAUDE.md §4, ruled 14z-111): a board crash is captured FIRST as a hand-played MAME recording on the same freeze, named `<what>-<freeze set>-NN` (the freeze it was PLAYED on), tracked in the repository, and replayed under an exception guard (a write tap on the game's own exception store, yielding vector, fault PC, registers and stack); every tracked recording is replayed at every freeze. Untracked, unnamed recordings are deleted, not kept. *(adapted: the original named `tests/inp/`, `tools/run_inp_guarded.sh` and `tests/test_inp_corpus.sh`.)*
- [MSV-35] **The board is a WITNESS, not the instrument, and a board crash is not evidence about the core.** The original's #99 — 100% on the board AND by hand on MAME — was game DATA (CPU-Phobos playing Demitri's AI through an aliased script table), never the RTL; three sessions of rig-derived mechanism preceded the first recording, which found it in an evening. Reproduce on MAME under the guard before any RTL theory.
- [MSV-36] **The field bundles live OUTSIDE the repo (rule 7, [VSR-8]) and their READMEs go stale**: a claim corrected in the tree does not reach an artifact that has already left it. When a claim is retracted, sweep the artifacts that have left the tree by hand ([VSR-33]); keep the card's instructions in an in-tree carrier (a MiSTer readme in the release). *(adapted: the original named its bundle directory beside the tree and `release/<name>/mister/MISTER.md`.)*
